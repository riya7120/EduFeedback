OUR PURPOSE

Feedback that creates
meaningful change.

CampusVoice is a demonstration Faculty Feedback and Course Evaluation Portal designed to make academic feedback organised, accessible and actionable.

CV
◎

Our mission

Provide a structured channel through which students can share experiences and contribute to better teaching and learning.

◇

Our vision

Encourage a culture of transparency, reflection and continuous improvement in higher education.

↗

Our approach

Collect structured ratings, thoughtful comments and visual summaries to help identify strengths and improvement opportunities.

Key objectives

  • Make faculty feedback easy to submit and review.
  • Evaluate teaching quality using consistent rating criteria.
  • Understand course-level student experiences.
  • Present feedback trends using charts and summaries.
  • Support evidence-informed academic improvement.

Academic project prototype

This website is a front-end demonstration built with HTML, CSS and JavaScript. It is not connected to a university server, student information system or secure database.

Frequently asked questions

How do I submit feedback?

Open Submit feedback, select a course and faculty member, provide ratings for all criteria, confirm the declaration and submit the form.

Can I submit anonymous feedback?

Yes. Select the anonymous option before submitting. Your name is omitted from the saved record in this browser demonstration.

Where can I see my previous submissions?

Open My feedback to view your saved submissions, ratings and dates.

How are the graphs calculated?

Charts use the average ratings from feedback records saved in the current browser. New submissions update the charts.

Can I use this portal on another device?

This prototype uses localStorage, so records do not automatically sync across devices or browsers.

Is this connected to a university database?

No. A production deployment would require a secure backend, database, authentication, access controls and privacy policies.
